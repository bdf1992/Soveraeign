# Projected reports v0.1

Status: `EXPERIMENTAL · ISSUE #200`

Reports are communication surfaces over one exact profile revision. They are not evidence and cannot acquire authority, identity, or validity by presentation quality.

## Native report

`sov-native-v0.1` preserves the experimental construct estimates directly. This is the default report surface because it adds the least theory beyond the observed/scored profile.

A useful human rendering should show, for each construct:

- the two semantic poles;
- center and spread;
- observation count and represented contexts;
- sufficiency/calibration standing;
- notable context shifts;
- omissions and contradictory evidence.

## Framework-like reports

`big-five-like-v0.1`, `mbti-like-v0.1`, and `cvi-like-v0.1` are deliberately unvalidated research mappings.

The deterministic tool requires explicit `--allow-unvalidated` to render them. They exist so that crosswalk hypotheses can be inspected, compared, and eventually defeated or calibrated from evidence.

They must never be described as official results of those instruments.

## Future reception report

A separate report should eventually answer a different question:

> How is this subject's expression likely to be perceived by this observer or cohort in this context?

Candidate graded dimensions include:

- warmth;
- agency/assertiveness;
- clarity;
- density;
- formality;
- playfulness;
- confidence presentation;
- friction;
- predictability;
- surprise/novelty.

Those dimensions should be researched independently. Some describe observable expression; others are explicitly observer reception. The report must preserve that distinction.

## Comparative language

Prefer statements shaped like:

- "Under the recorded reversible-uncertainty probes, this subject gathered additional evidence more often than it committed immediately."
- "The profile has a high center and low spread on invariant-fidelity under the contexts observed."
- "The CVI-like Innovator projection is high, but the mapping is currently UNVALIDATED and should be read as an archetype hypothesis rather than a CVI score."

Avoid statements shaped like:

- "This code is an INTJ."
- "The model is 82nd-percentile conscientious" when no admitted comparable cohort exists.
- "This person is objectively abrasive" when the evidence is observer reception.

The goal is not to remove evocative language. It is to make evocative language resolvable back to evidence, methodology, uncertainty, and point of view.
