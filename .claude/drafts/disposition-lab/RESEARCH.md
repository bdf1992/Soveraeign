# Disposition Lab research anchors

Status: `RESEARCH NOTE · ISSUE #200`

These sources constrain the experiment. They do not ratify the seed construct bank or any projection mapping.

## Test quality and intended use

**AERA, APA, NCME — Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing (2014).**

Use: treat validity as evidence supporting a score interpretation for a proposed use, alongside reliability/precision and fairness. Disposition Lab therefore cannot call an instrument generally "valid" without naming the use and population.

Sources:
- https://www.apa.org/science/programs/testing/standards
- https://www.testingstandards.net/open-access-files.html

## Measurement equivalence

**Putnick, D. L., & Bornstein, M. H. (2016). Measurement Invariance Conventions and Reporting: The State of the Art and Future Directions for Psychological Research. Developmental Review, 41, 71–90.**

Use: meaningful group/time comparisons require evidence that the construct retains comparable psychometric meaning. This is the basis for Disposition Lab's default `NOT_COMPARABLE` crossing between subject kinds/adapters.

Source:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5145197/

## Traits as distributions of states

**Fleeson, W., & Jayawickreme, E. (2015). Whole Trait Theory. Journal of Research in Personality, 56, 82–92.**

Use: descriptive traits can be represented as density distributions of momentary states, preserving stable differences in distribution parameters alongside substantial within-person variation. This motivates reporting center + spread + context rather than one naked score.

Source:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4472377/

**Fleeson, W., & Law, M. K. (2015). Trait enactments as density distributions: The role of actors, situations, and observers in explaining stability and variability. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 109(6), 1090–1104.**

Use: observable trait enactments remain highly variable; situation and observer effects should be represented rather than discarded as mere noise.

Source:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4673017/

## Adaptive personality assessment

**Waller, N. G., & Reise, S. P. (1989). Computerized adaptive personality assessment: an illustration with the Absorption scale. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 57(6), 1051–1058.**

Use: establishes early feasibility of IRT-based adaptive personality assessment and substantial item reduction under calibrated conditions.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2614658/

**Forbey, J. D., & Ben-Porath, Y. S. (2007). Computerized adaptive personality testing: a review and illustration with the MMPI-2 Computerized Adaptive Version. Psychological Assessment, 19(1), 14–24.**

Use: supports adaptive item selection as an efficiency mechanism, but only after an instrument has a defensible calibrated bank.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17371120/

## Open research items

**International Personality Item Pool (IPIP).**

Use: public-domain pool of more than 3,000 items and more than 250 scales. Suitable for early human research without cloning proprietary item banks.

Source:
- https://ipip.ori.org/

## Established dimensional inventories

**Lee, K., & Ashton, M. C. (2018). Psychometric Properties of the HEXACO-100. Assessment, 25(5), 543–556.**

Use: example of the level of empirical work a serious dimensional inventory requires: large samples, factor structure, facet structure, and self/observer convergence.

Source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27411678/

**Moshagen et al./related reliability literature should be considered before treating internal consistency as sufficient reliability evidence.** Short-term retest estimates and cross-rater agreement are useful complementary evidence.

Example source:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35025932/

## LLM psychometrics

**A psychometric framework for evaluating and shaping personality traits in large language models. Nature Machine Intelligence (2025).**

Use: demonstrates that model personality-like measurement requires controlled response variation, repeated psychometric testing, and external construct-validity checks. Prompt/preamble/postamble configuration is part of the measurement procedure and therefore must be pinned in a Soveraeign model subject revision.

Source:
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01115-6

## Research interpretation

These anchors support the architecture, not the eight seed dimensions. The core research obligation remains empirical:

- does each probe exercise the construct it claims to exercise?
- are profile parameters repeatable under pinned conditions?
- what variance is subject-driven versus context/adapter-driven?
- what factor structure actually appears?
- which established-framework crosswalks survive held-out testing?
- which subject/adapter crossings earn measurement equivalence, partial equivalence, or refusal?

A surprising negative result is preferable to a polished but unfalsifiable personality narrative.
