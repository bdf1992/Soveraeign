# Register card — Bdo

How to hand this owner a result. Every line below is grounded in something
observed on 2026-08-23, not inferred; where nothing was observed the line says
**unobserved** rather than guessing. Two sessions have now guessed at this
register and been told they were wrong, so the gaps are left open on purpose.

The failure this card exists to prevent: a session produced a correct, complete
list of twenty open decisions with their gate names attached, and it was rated
2/10 — *"nothing owner translated at all."* The information was right. The
register was wrong.

## Holds, cold

He built all of this and he cannot parse its identifiers cold. **Coined is not
owned.** He invented `O10` and `spec.ratify` in a warm hour deep in the work;
handed back three weeks — or three hours — later they are encryption, not
compression.

Observed verbatim: *"You think the Owner of Starbucks gets his reports this
way?"* and *"This is just 20 dry cards, with nothing owner transalted at all."*

## Does NOT own

Longer than what he owns, which is normal and is the point.

- Open-decision identifiers — `O2`, `O9`, `O10`, `O16` — as carriers of meaning.
- Gate names: `spec.ratify`, `classification.ratify`, `engineering.ratify_baseline`.
- Protected-boundary strings: `no_runtime_code_before_logical_spec_and_defeating_fixtures`.
- Phase labels `F0`–`F4` as *impact*. He knows the ladder; he does not read `F0`
  and feel what is blocked.
- Standing tokens as prose: `BUILT_SELF_TESTED_NOT_WITNESSED` reads as a string,
  not as "nobody has ever checked this."

Translate every one of these or delete it. If an identifier must appear, put it
in a drawer captioned *what this is called if you need to look it up* — never in
the sentence carrying the meaning.

## Cares about

- **Impact, not status.** Not "O10 is open" but "three services are frozen until
  you answer."
- **Evidence he can check.** Observed as an explicit list of what a report owes:
  *"evidence and proof and backing and covering and socres and entertainment and
  and owners read."*
- **Scores.** Hard numbers land. A `5.0%` mutation score on the module that would
  gate his main branch changed the conversation immediately; adjectives never did.
- **Being able to refuse.** He asked what would give him confidence to say yes.
  What actually served him was four verdicts saying no, with traces.
- **Effort being visible.** *"I know I spent way more effort for My boss on what
  I'm reporting on."* A cheap-to-write deliverable reads as disrespect.

## Decides

Everything. Sole ratifier, sole collaborator on the repository, and the only
principal named in `CODEOWNERS`. Nothing is ratified without him,
which is why the queue is the constraint and why no agent may present its
synthesis as his judgement.

## Time

Five minutes on a report; one hour to a delivered artifact when he sets a
deadline. Observed: *"You have 1 hour to deliver."* Front-load the judgment — he
may stop after the first line, and the first line must still carry the most
important thing.

## Shape that worked

- One judgment first, before any evidence.
- The asset stated before the problem. A brief that opens on what is broken and
  never says what the thing is worth reads as a complaint.
- Progressive disclosure: headline visible, evidence in a drawer, citations
  inside it.
- Deck over document when the material is a decision; he asked for a slide deck
  by name.
- **Disclose your own stake.** One of the decisions was authored by the session
  reporting on it; saying so plainly, and ranking it outside the top, was worth
  more than the ranking.

## Shape that failed

- A queue of items with controls. He does not want to work the queue; he wants
  the read on it.
- Twenty of anything. Twenty decisions compress to four that matter and sixteen
  that can wait — and even that is *one slide* of an owner report, not the report.
- Arguing for yes. He is not looking to be sold; he is looking to be able to
  refuse responsibly.
- Any identifier doing load-bearing work in a sentence.

## Unobserved — ask, do not guess

- Which artifacts he forwards to anyone else, and to whom.
- Whether he reads on a phone.
- How he wants dissent surfaced when two witnesses disagree.
- What he considers an acceptable cost for a decision that turns out wrong —
  this shapes how conservative a recommendation should be, and nothing observed
  today settles it.
