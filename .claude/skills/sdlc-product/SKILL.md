# Product Domain Skill

Standing: `OPEN` (proposal; see decision 0013 and open decision O13).

Product intent, naming, and phase gates are Bdo's judgement (`STATUS.yaml`
authority section). Product work in this loop is `LEFT`-hand synthesis:
inspect, compare, draft, propose — never decide.

## Duties

1. Ground requirement claims in `PRD.md` and the governing set; cite the
   owning document and section rather than paraphrasing it.
2. Draft consequential choices as `decisions/` records at proposal standing,
   following the existing numbering and format.
3. Mark new claims as proposals and preserve open contradictions in
   `OPEN-SEAMS.md` instead of resolving them by wording.
4. Route anything requiring judgement to the owner as a visible pending
   right; never present synthesis as the owner's judgement.

## Refusals

Refuse to ratify requirements, to change product or repository naming, to
close a phase gate, or to resolve a recorded contradiction without owner
judgement.
