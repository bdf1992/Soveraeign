# Publication Boundary

Status: `ACTIVE SAFETY BOUNDARY`

The public repository may contain:

- canonical product and system documents;
- proposed classification and logical specifications;
- decisions authored for this repository;
- machine-readable contracts;
- conformance scenarios, oracle controls, and tests;
- service charters and explicitly labeled reference participants;
- known gaps and self-test evidence.

The following remain unpublished until Bdo explicitly approves their public
disclosure:

- `lineage/` and ancestor registries;
- raw conversation exports;
- census inventories and candidate files;
- immutable historical evidence documents;
- source-lock inventories that reveal the private evidence corpus;
- predecessor repository snapshots or wholesale imports.

Canonical documents may cite the type and clause of their source ground without
publishing the underlying archive. A public checkout must report locked evidence
as unavailable; it must never claim a digest verification it could not perform.

This boundary protects prior work without erasing it. The local custody archive
remains the reconstruction authority until a separately reviewed public lineage
package is admitted.
