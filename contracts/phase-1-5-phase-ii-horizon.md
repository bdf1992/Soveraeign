# Prepared Phase 1.5 / Phase II Horizon

Status: `PREPARED · HUMAN-READABLE · NO PHASE STANDING`

This record exists so a person can understand the intended relationship between
Phase 1.5 and Phase II before any successor phase is opened. It is a prepared
reading of the current product, logical specification, and the design work that
followed the closed Phase-I campaign. It does not open a phase, change standing,
carry authority, or make Phase II active.

`STATUS.yaml` and `contracts/phases.json` remain authoritative for phase state.
While they say `NONE_ACTIVE` and name no successor, no text in this file may be
read as permission to perform successor-phase work. A future root opening act may
choose to pin this record as part of a phase definition; until that act happens,
this is preparation only.

## The horizon in one sentence

**Phase 1.5 commissions the trustworthy primitives and composition rules that
Phase II citizens need in order to organize themselves, operate institutions and
nodes, federate without collapsing sovereignty, learn from recorded experience,
and produce evidence-backed candidates for what Soveraeign should undertake
next.**

That is the reason Phase 1.5 exists. It is not an infrastructure intermission and
it is not a small version of Phase II.

## What Phase I left us

Phase I established the founding vocabulary, ground, contracts, service shapes,
authority distinctions, Record semantics, and a substantial amount of working
machinery. It closed `CLOSED_INCOMPLETE` because the campaign did not keep its
own exit predicates on the critical path of work. The lesson is not that the
foundation was useless. The lesson is that component progress is not enough:
Soveraeign must prove complete governed circuits and keep the evidence for those
circuits attached to the work that is meant to close them.

The gap after Phase I therefore removes reconciliation that should not leak into
successor work: historical material must remain historical, principal and session
boundaries must be explicit, consequential history must converge into Record,
and a fresh participant must be able to discover what is current without
reconstructing the system from archaeology or oral history.

## Phase 1.5 · Operational Commissioning

Working name: **Participant Delivery Substrate**.

Phase 1.5 is the commissioning campaign between a founded system and a society of
participants. Its job is to prove that the lower-level Soveraeign mechanisms
compose into useful, fair, inspectable participant behavior without requiring a
privileged model, private controller memory, or a hardcoded institution for every
new kind of work.

The campaign commissions these kinds of primitives and relations:

- durable principal identity and isolated session provenance;
- capability discovery separated from authority;
- typed, scoped, revocable authority and bounded effect envelopes;
- durable work, agenda, custody, leases, closure and cleanup;
- versioned Assets and append-preserving operational Record;
- reconstructable, role-appropriate `RecordProjection` readings of common
  history;
- cited `Finding` objects whose subject, scope, evidence and evaluator relation
  are explicit;
- independent observation and findings that can be frozen before comparison;
- fair comparison that preserves disagreement and attributes defects to the
  correct subject instead of averaging opinions;
- explicit settlement, receipts, cleanup and fresh discovery;
- definition recurrence: settled experience can become cited input to a later
  Proposal or candidate Definition without becoming authority merely by being
  generated;
- institution-neutral composition: the same primitives can support forms of
  organization the founder did not have to predict.

The current Controller, Orchestrator, Worker and Witness roles are useful because
they are a demanding first institution with distinct subjects of judgement. They
are **commissioning instances**, not the ontology of Soveraeign citizenship.

A Worker can carry an assignment well while the Work still fails. Work can
succeed while the participant carrying it violates authority or hides material
failure. An Orchestrator may judge the participant-in-work, while an independent
Witness judges the work and observable result. A Controller may compare their
frozen, cited findings and classify the disagreement. Record preserves the basis
for all of those readings; Record decides none of them.

That distinction is important because the substrate must remain useful when
Phase II citizens invent different roles and institutions.

## The proving circuit

The human shape of the Phase 1.5 commissioning circuit is:

```text
Definition
  -> Request
  -> Agenda
  -> Queue / Custody / Lease
  -> Participant Context
       - identity + session
       - capability
       - authority
       - governance context
       - RecordProjection
  -> Execution
  -> operational Record + report
  -> independent perspective formation
       - participant-in-work Finding
       - work Finding
  -> frozen Findings
  -> comparison / repair / decision
  -> Settlement
  -> cleanup + Receipt
  -> Discovery
  -> Use
  -> Experience
  -> Synthesis
  -> candidate next Definition
```

The final candidate Definition is deliberately **unprivileged**. It may be
better informed than the original definition and may be supported by extensive
evidence. None of that gives it phase standing, policy authority, or permission
to rewrite the rule that produced it.

The mechanics hold the letter. Agency may interpret how best to serve intent
inside valid authority. Record makes that judgement inspectable. Authority
remains the thing that decides what becomes binding.

## What Phase 1.5 must not become

Phase 1.5 must not pre-build the institutions Phase II is supposed to discover.
It therefore does not need a special Council service, Institution service,
Federation-of-Agents hierarchy, worker reputation system, planner database, or a
new authority species merely because one imaginable Phase II organization might
use one.

When a higher-order capability appears to be missing, the commissioning question
is:

> **What lower-level governed primitive, relation, projection, receipt, cutoff or
> citation edge is actually missing from the composition?**

If the answer is a small primitive, add the small primitive. If the proposed
solution hardcodes one future institution, it belongs in Phase II unless the
commissioning circuit proves that the substrate itself cannot work without it.

## Phase II · Citizenship

Phase II is where the commissioned substrate is used rather than further
specified for its own sake.

Citizens may carry their own agendas, compose capabilities, establish teams or
institutions, operate sovereign nodes, create their own work and decision
arrangements, hand custody across boundaries, and federate with other sovereign
nodes without collapsing ownership into one central participant.

Examples of things Phase II citizens may discover and create include:

- a procurement institution with its own roles and decision rules;
- a production institution with a different internal topology;
- a compliance or audit institution whose perspective is intentionally
  independent of both;
- temporary project teams assembled around an agenda;
- user-authored workflows and offices;
- sovereign nodes with locally chosen operating arrangements;
- crossings between institutions or nodes with explicit authority, custody,
  evidence and settlement;
- new skills, services, Definitions and policy candidates learned from actual
  use.

Those examples are not Phase II requirements merely because they are written
here. Their purpose is to show why Phase 1.5 must be institution-neutral.

The Phase II question is not "did we build more substrate?" It is:

> **What can citizens accomplish, organize and learn now that trustworthy
> participation exists?**

## How Phase II can create pressure for a later phase

A healthy Phase II should be capable of helping discover why a later phase needs
to exist.

For example, independently operated institutions or nodes may repeatedly produce
records and findings showing that a particular cross-node custody rule,
federation capability, delegation pattern, or evidence property is inadequate.
Those experiences can be composed into a cited candidate requirement:

```text
recorded operations
  + participant findings
  + witness findings
  + settlements
  + repeated scoped tensions
        -> synthesis
        -> candidate later-phase Definition
```

That candidate may eventually become part of a Phase III definition or some
other future campaign. The number and name are not important here, and no future
phase is opened or promised by this example.

The boundary is important:

- citizens may discover a need;
- citizens may define it;
- citizens may support it with exact evidence;
- citizens may make reversible demonstrations where their authority allows;
- citizens may propose it through the ordinary authority path;
- **citizens may not grant the proposal standing they do not hold.**

This generalizes the same rule used for principled deviation: successful
experience may create a policy candidate, but experience does not automatically
change policy.

## Sov and composed operating intelligence

Sov — the participant together with the agent helping them operate — should not
become a fifth bureaucratic workflow tier. It is the operating intelligence made
possible by composing the same scoped Record and Findings available to the
participant.

A Sov context may combine, without collapsing them:

- current phase and reachable work;
- Controller comparison and settlement state;
- Orchestrator findings about how an assignment was carried;
- Worker reports and operational records;
- Witness findings about the work itself;
- unresolved disagreement and missing evidence;
- available capabilities and actual authority;
- repeated tensions from prior settled circuits.

The result is an evidence-backed answer to questions such as "what should we do
next?" or "what definition should change?" Every material claim remains
expandable to its cited basis. Sov may synthesize; synthesis is not authority.

A useful compression is:

> **Agency learns. Record remembers. Authority decides what becomes binding.**

## The boundary between the campaigns

The intended relationship is therefore:

```text
Phase I
  founded the system and exposed the qualification lesson

Gap / staging
  removes inherited reconciliation and makes the next campaign startable

Phase 1.5
  proves trustworthy participation, evidence composition and learning recurrence

Phase II
  citizens use those capabilities to form institutions, operate nodes and do
  meaningful self-directed work

Later phase candidates
  may be discovered from the recorded experience of those citizens
```

Or, more compactly:

> **Gap removes inherited friction. Phase 1.5 proves participation. Phase II
> exercises citizenship. Later phases may be informed by what citizens actually
> learn.**

## What would justify opening Phase 1.5

Opening Phase 1.5 is a separate root act. Preparation is sufficient when a fresh
participant can, from the repository alone:

1. establish principal and isolated session context;
2. discover that no phase is currently active and identify the explicit next
   gate;
3. read this prepared horizon and the Phase 1.5 qualification profile without
   mistaking either for active standing;
4. resolve the prepared commissioning predicates and their evidence machinery;
5. discover the custody/work mechanism that will carry the phase exit once
   opened;
6. distinguish what is gap prerequisite work from what belongs inside the
   commissioning campaign;
7. demonstrate that the verification path will measure active-phase progress
   rather than allow another campaign to drift on component counts.

At that point root may choose to open Phase 1.5 by an explicit change to the
phase authority records. The opening should pin the exact definition bytes it is
opening against and create live custody for every unmet exit clause immediately.

Until that happens, this document remains what its status says: a human-readable
prepared horizon, with **no phase standing**.
