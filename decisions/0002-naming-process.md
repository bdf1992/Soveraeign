# 0002 · Naming process

Status: `SUPERSEDED BY 0004`

## Decision

Keep both product and repository proper names OPEN during founding closure. Use
descriptive metadata and the bootstrap artifact name only as working handles.

A product name may land only after the system boundary is frozen, candidates
are tested against `NAMING.md`, current collision checks are performed, and Bdo
makes the selection.

Owner disposition: Bdo selected `Soveraeign` directly while authorizing the
founding repository. Decision 0004 records the current state. Collision
screening remains open, but it no longer prevents repository establishment.

## Reason

A premature name becomes an architectural attractor. It can overemphasize the
substrate, a subsystem, a metaphor, or a predecessor and then cause the project to
conform to the name rather than the intended enterprise capability.

## Historical acceptance before supersession

- `STATUS.yaml` stated `product_name: OPEN` and `repository_name: OPEN` until an
  owner selection landed.
- Candidate research does not rename files or packages.
- The selected name receives its own decision record with alternatives,
  collision evidence, owner sign, and migration steps.
