# 0005 · Operational qualification language

Status: `OWNER-DIRECTED`

## Decision

Replace the canonical “birth” metaphor with the literal operational condition
it represented:

> the first independently reproducible, owner-accepted operating state that
> satisfies the Phase-I contract.

Canonical terms are now:

| Previous metaphor | Canonical term |
| --- | --- |
| pre-birth | founding or pre-implementation |
| birth condition / birth gate | Phase-I operational qualification |
| birth witness | independent qualification witness |
| birth decision | Phase-I operational acceptance |
| nothing is born true | no claim enters as authoritative |
| clean-room birth | clean-room operational qualification |

## Consequences

- Qualification remains evidence-gated and independently witnessed.
- Bdo retains the judgement authority for operational acceptance.
- Passing tests alone does not qualify the system when their evidence cannot be
  reconstructed from the artifact.
- Historical evidence remains byte-identical and may retain the earlier
  metaphor; canonical documents translate it rather than rewriting history.
