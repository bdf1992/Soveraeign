# 0006 · AI-native standard

Status: `OWNER-DIRECTED · FREEZE CANDIDATE`

## Decision

Soveraeign will use an opinionated, falsifiable AI-native definition rather
than the market label “uses AI.”

The standard has two layers:

1. the already-grounded minimum per-surface threshold: reachability is a gate,
   at least one of commitment/provenance/retraction is partial or better, and
   the AI operation must be substantive;
2. the stricter Soveraeign qualification: same-world parity, typed authority,
   independent observation, receipt completeness, effect honesty, and measured
   cold-start competence.

The full predicates and record schema live in `AI-NATIVE.md`.

## Policy boundary

The second layer is new canonical Soveraeign policy derived from the founding
contract. It does not retroactively change the earlier minimum arithmetic in
the locked source corpus.

The exact `NONE`/`PARTIAL`/`FULL` meanings, `INVALID`/`OPEN` assessment states,
all-`FULL` qualification bar, two-binding proof, and integration-loss
sovereignty remain freeze-candidate policy until Bdo ratifies or strikes them.

## Consequence for the Asset Service

The Asset Service's first vertical slice must be evaluated as a named asset operation. An
agent integration, chat surface, graph visualization, or generated derivative
does not count by presence alone.
