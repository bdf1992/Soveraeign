# 0007 · Asset Service boundary

Status: `SOURCE-GROUNDED PROPOSAL`

## Decision

Build digital asset management as Soveraeign's first Asset Service and one concrete
local reference binding. It may expose a microservice deployment boundary while
remaining governed by the same authoritative transitions as every other
service.

Federation readiness is required at the interface boundary. Federation itself
is not claimed until a distinct second sovereign node participates in a
governed crossing.
