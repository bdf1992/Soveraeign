# 0011 · Local, personal, and BYOM pattern

Status: `OWNER-DIRECTED · CONTRACT WORDING PROPOSED`

## Decision

Treat a personally owned local node as the first-class starting pattern for
Soveraeign. Personal, team, and enterprise are operating profiles of the same
node contract rather than different products or architecture tiers.

Adopt Bring Your Own Model as the model practice: the owner may configure a
compatible local or remote model through a declared Model Binding and Model
Adapter without changing authoritative state, service contracts, standing,
typed authority, provenance, receipts, or retraction semantics.

Add model portability as a Phase-I requirement and conformance surface.
