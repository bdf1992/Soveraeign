## Where it fizzled

1. **A grant on the attempt makes independence unanswerable.** The Observation Service walks
   `GRANT_DESCENDS_FROM_RUN` from `GRANT` entries in the journal. The journal holds none for
   `grant:standing-landing-loop`, which lives in `contracts/standing-grants.json`, and holds no
   lease entry for the candidate observer, so a grantless witness reads as unanswerable and the
   relation is `UNDETERMINED`. The thin circuit was observable only because its `grant_id` was
   null. Q1.3 and Q3.1 therefore trade against each other under the current rule: name the grant
   and lose the observation, or omit it and lose the authority. The record needs the grant
   issuance and the lease as journal entries, or a service default that reads a candidate with
   no recorded grant as answerable. `services/observation/KNOWN-GAPS.md` records the current
   reading as a default taken; overturning it is the observation domain's, and the journal
   entries are the record domain's. Held at `DEPENDENCY_SEAM` for those two artifacts.
2. **The journal does not say who appended.** The controller appended the worker's `OUTPUT` and
   `REPORTED` entries under the worker's actor id. The orchestrator could not attest who wrote
   them (claim 04). Fizzle 3 of the thin circuit in its exact form.
3. **The hypervisor is Windows-bound.** It launches through Windows Terminal and PowerShell, so
   on this host a fresh participant is again an agent in the same clone.
4. **Verify does not run the clarity check.** `verify.py` fails on the stale docs page a
   gap-list edit causes and not on the stale receipt; `sov_clarity.py check` is a separate
   command the landing runs. Witness residual RS-3.
5. **The R11 run-id test pins the guard but not the hazard.** Removing the guard yields
   `PredicatesUndeclared`, not a false observation, because the fixture's other run reports
   nothing. Witness RS-2; the test stays as a guard pin.
6. **Two helpers on one reference still collide**, and a fifth helper lease on this concern
   would have exceeded the emission ceiling of four, so the settlement of this run was the
   controller's own act; it neither reported nor observed the run, so no self-settlement.
